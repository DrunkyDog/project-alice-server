4. Service Content

4.1 Service Overview: This Service relies on artificial intelligence technology, calls third-party generative AI models through API interfaces, and provides intelligent interaction services for connected hardware devices, including but not limited to Voice Activity Detection (VAD), Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), Semantic Understanding (LLM), Text-to-Speech (TTS), Visual Understanding (VLLM), Intent Recognition, Conversation Memory, Knowledge Base (RAG), etc.

4.2 AI Agent Management: You can create and configure AI agents through the Management Console, including setting role templates, selecting language models, configuring voice parameters, setting intent recognition rules, managing knowledge bases, enabling conversation memory, configuring AI agent plugins, etc. The settings of AI agents must not violate national laws and regulations.

4.3 Device Management: You can bind and manage your hardware devices through the Management Console, performing device configuration, firmware updates, OTA remote upgrades, and other operations. You should ensure that the devices you manage are legally owned by you or have been authorized.

4.4 Visual Understanding: This Service supports collecting images through device cameras and calling third-party visual models for image recognition and scene understanding. You should ensure that image collection complies with relevant laws and regulations and does not infringe on others' privacy.

4.5 Intent Recognition: This Service can understand the intent of user instructions through intent recognition functionality and trigger corresponding operations or services, such as controlling smart devices, querying information, etc.

4.6 Conversation Memory: This Service can record and store summaries of your interaction history with AI agents to provide more coherent and personalized interaction experiences in subsequent conversations. You can manage or clear memory data in the Management Console.

4.7 Knowledge Base: This Service supports you uploading documents, texts, and other content to build knowledge bases. AI agents can retrieve knowledge base content to provide Q&A services in conversations. You should ensure that uploaded knowledge base content does not infringe third-party intellectual property rights and does not contain content that violates laws and regulations.

4.8 Voice Print Recognition: This Service supports voice print recognition functionality. You can register voice print samples to enable speaker identity verification and personalized services. Voice print data will be used for identity verification purposes.

4.9 Voice Cloning: This Service supports voice cloning functionality. You can clone custom voice tones by providing audio samples. Cloned voice tones are for your personal use only and may not be used for impersonation or illegal activities.

4.10 MCP Endpoint: This Service supports the MCP (Model Context Protocol) protocol and can serve as an MCP Server to provide tool calling capabilities externally, or as an MCP Client to call external MCP services to achieve standardized integration with external systems.

4.11 Context Provider: This Service supports Context Provider functionality to obtain real-time information from external data sources, such as weather, news, stocks, etc., to provide AI agents with richer knowledge sources.

4.12 MQTT Protocol: This Service supports MQTT (Message Queuing Telemetry Transport) protocol for message communication between IoT devices. You can implement device control command transmission, device status monitoring, and other functions through MQTT protocol.

4.13 Service Description: The dialogue and reply content generated by third-party AI models relied upon by this Service is for reference only and does not constitute any professional advice. You may not use the output content as professional advice in medical, legal, financial, or other fields. You bear full responsibility for any judgments or decisions made based on the output content.
