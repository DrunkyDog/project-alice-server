1. Information We Collect

To provide our Service, we may need to collect the following information:

1.1 Account Registration Information: When you register an account, we will collect your phone number or username, password, and other information for creating and verifying your account.

1.2 Device Information: When you bind hardware devices, we will collect device identification information (such as device codes, MAC addresses, etc.), device models, firmware versions, etc., for device management and service provision.

1.3 AI Agent Configuration Information: When you create and configure AI agents, we will collect information such as role templates, language model choices, and voice parameter configurations you set, to provide personalized AI interactive services.

1.4 Voice Interaction Data: When you use the voice interaction feature, this Service will use Voice Activity Detection (VAD) to determine your voice start and end states, and process your voice input data by transmitting it to third-party Speech Recognition (ASR) and Language Model (LLM) services for processing to implement voice interaction functionality.

1.5 Image Data: When you use the visual model functionality, we may process image data collected through your device camera and transmit it to third-party visual model services for analysis and understanding, to implement image recognition, scene understanding, and other functions.

1.6 Conversation Memory Data: When you enable the memory feature, this Service will store summaries of your interaction history with AI agents to provide more coherent and personalized interaction experiences in subsequent conversations.

1.7 Knowledge Base Data: When you use the knowledge base feature, we will collect and store knowledge base content such as documents and texts you upload for AI agents to retrieve knowledge and provide answers in conversations.

1.8 Voice Print Data: When you use the voice print recognition feature, we will collect and store your voice print characteristic samples for speaker identity verification and personalized services.

1.9 Chat History Data: We will store your conversation history with AI agents, including text conversation content, conversation time, interaction context, and other information to provide continuous conversation experience and history query functionality.

1.10 Conversation Audio Data: When you use the voice interaction feature, we may store audio data of your interactions with AI agents for optimizing voice interaction quality and historical queries.

1.11 AI Agent Configuration Data: When you configure AI agents, we will collect information about tags, plugin configurations, context provider settings, and other settings you set to provide customized AI agent services.

1.12 Device Firmware Data: When you use the OTA upgrade feature, we will record device firmware versions, upgrade history, and other information for device management and firmware traceability.

1.13 Log Information: When you use this Service, we will automatically collect service log information, including but not limited to access time, IP address, browser type, operation records, etc., for service operation and security assurance.

1.14 Verification Code Information: When you log in using your phone number, we will send verification codes to you through SMS service for identity verification.

2. How We Use Collected Information

The information we collect will be used for the following purposes:

(1) Provide, maintain, and improve this Service, including core functions such as account management, device management, and AI agent configuration.

(2) Process your voice interaction requests, identify voice input through Voice Activity Detection (VAD), and call third-party AI model services to complete speech recognition, intent recognition, semantic understanding, and speech synthesis.

(3) Process your image data and call third-party visual model services to complete image recognition and scene understanding.

(4) Store and manage your conversation memory data to provide more coherent service experience in subsequent interactions.

(5) Store and retrieve knowledge base content you uploaded so that AI agents can provide you with more accurate knowledge-based answers in conversations.

(6) Ensure the security and stability of the Service, including identity verification, security protection, troubleshooting, etc.

(7) Comply with applicable laws, regulations, and regulatory requirements.

We will not use your personal information for other purposes unrelated to the above purposes. If we need to use information for other purposes not stated in this Privacy Policy, we will obtain your consent in advance.
